About Finastra

Finastra is a global leader in financial services technology, delivering innovative solutions to banks, financial institutions, and fintech companies worldwide.

With a focus on open finance, AI innovation, and collaborative ecosystems, we're driving the future of financial services. Our annual conference brings together the brightest minds in the industry to shape tomorrow's financial landscape.
